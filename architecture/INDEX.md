## Contracts

- **artifact-lineage:** Every tested or published userscript is bound to an exact source SHA and SHA-256.
- **filter-semantics:** Mobile and PC preserve filtering decisions, personal blocks, identity parsing, and delayed-content coverage.
- **host-dom-events:** Site-owned nodes, forms, native handlers, popup descendants, order, and submission paths remain intact.
- **initialization-lifecycle:** Document-start locking, overlay release, duplicate guard, pageshow recovery, and teardown form one lifecycle.
- **metadata-scope:** Board-route matches, grants, noframes, and document-start timing remain unchanged unless explicitly approved.
- **proof-integrity:** Evidence binds SUT, oracle, fixture, harness, toolchain, route, and complete result status.
- **storage-compatibility:** GM keys, readable legacy shapes, defaults, and round trips remain compatible.
- **target-isolation:** PC receives shared filter and management UI without mobile host layout or theme CSS.
- **ui-ownership:** Each surface has one final visual owner; presentation consumes snapshots and emits intents only.
