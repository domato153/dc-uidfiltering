# DCUF modernization decision rationale

## D-23 — proportionate verification, CI and continuity

- SELECTED: the researched implementation in `VERIFICATION_EFFICIENCY.md`: runtime/verification relation separation, conservative shared/unknown fallback, bounded header coverage, selected mutation groups with exact-input retry and fresh preflight, failed-prerequisite blocking with restoration, risk-selected CI and pnpm download caching, coherent checkpoint units, typed artifact identities and direct-action continuity. This governs method, not a product PASS.
- NOT ADOPTED: arbitrary command/result deduplication, browser caching against Playwright guidance, untrusted cross-environment or cross-head pass reuse, blanket CI skipping, dummy commits for tests, or deleting known failure/permission boundaries to shrink context.
- DEFERRED: migration of all historical globally bound stage receipts to per-feature dependency identities; narrowing those without producer/consumer proof could create false reuse. An unrelated continuity fingerprint alone never justifies unchanged product tests.
- Reason: Current routes confused verification dependencies with product impact, repeated full audits and focused coverage, and made local testing appear to require commits. Narrow selection and exact-input recovery remove those costs while retaining conservative runtime fallbacks and fresh controls. Compact current-state retrieval keeps decision-critical boundaries without making historical traces mandatory.
- Evidence: `docs/work/VERIFICATION_EFFICIENCY.md` retains primary research, transfer limits and actual results; `tools/test-verification-efficiency.mjs` tests routing/execution/recovery/CI adversarially. All six proof-audit groups ran because the audit harness changed; continuity closure and same-input resume ran separately. These process results do not close known header FAIL/UNKNOWN.
- Revisit: Scoped coverage misses a real changed dependency, runtime/baseline/toolchain identity changes, profiling shows preparation still dominates, or a cold receiver cannot derive one valid next task without unlisted historical prerequisites.

This is a compact record of consequential choices and alternatives, not a second current-state or architecture authority. `SELECTED` means chosen for the present candidate/workflow, **not** product acceptance or a PASS receipt. `NOT ADOPTED` applies to this scope, not every future situation. `DEFERRED` stays unresolved until its stated trigger. Fresh Git/artifact state, machine contracts, receipts, `CURRENT_STATE.md`, and `NEXT_TASK.md` outrank this rationale.

The format adapts the explicit “adapted / not adopted wholesale” separation in the read-only `translation:main@0aab6011` `.agents/skills/maintenance-regression-supervisor/references/source-notes.md`, plus the short context/decision/consequence and supersession principle of [Nygard's ADR proposal](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions). Do not copy its project authority or create a chronological command diary here. A changed decision needs a new or explicitly superseding entry; do not silently relabel prior rationale as a verified result.
